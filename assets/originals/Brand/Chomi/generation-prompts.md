# Chomi icon artwork

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, transparent background enabled for every output.
Identity and drawing-style reference: `chomi-reference.png`, supplied by the user.
The existing 25 sticker images are outside this replacement.

The generated PNGs replace the existing files in `src/Vellume.App/Assets/Brand/` so existing icon consumers keep their paths. `scripts/build-app-icon.ps1` derives the 16–256 px ICO frames from the app PNG without changing the artwork.

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset: one Windows desktop application mascot icon, a single square transparent PNG. Use the supplied CHOMI character sheet as the identity AND drawing-style reference. Draw only Chomi's front-facing head and upper chest, centered and large, with a soft pale mint circular badge behind her and transparent pixels outside the circle. This is a new app icon, no text, no labels, no contact sheet. Preserve the reference's rounded very-short chibi face, half-lidded pink/magenta eyes with pale yellow heart highlights, tiny catlike smile, blush, pale ice-aqua twin tails with black under-hair, upward loop ahoge, simple dark brown/black ribbons with braided hanging ties, stacked pink bunny / mint ice-cream scoop / tiny cone hair ornament on viewer right, white hooded jacket with dark brown cuffs and aqua sailor bow. Keep the reference's clean dark outline, restrained flat pastel shading and small white hair highlights; no painterly texture or extra glossy rendering. Tight icon framing: head occupies about 75 percent of canvas, cropped twin-tail ends and chest are fine; keep ahoge and both ribbons visible. Strong readable silhouette at 32px. Do not import any clothing or jewelry from other characters. No lettering or watermark. Output one icon only.
